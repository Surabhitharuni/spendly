Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly experience:

  • To display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To maintain your account and authenticate your sessions.
  • To identify and fix bugs or performance issues in the app.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your expense data is associated with your account and is not accessible to other users. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

We retain your data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at:

  • Email: privacy@spendly.app

We aim to respond to all privacy-related queries within 5 business days.
